Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 1, 2026

Find ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Find mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the App. Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Phone number — used for SMS one-time-password (OTP) authentication.
  • Name — your first and last name, provided during onboarding.
  • Profile photo or avatar — an uploaded photo or one of our default emoji avatars.
  • Bio — an optional short description you may add to your profile.
  • Interests — the topic categories you select (e.g., science, design, culture) to personalize your daily finds.

Usage Data

  • Find interactions — which finds you open, read, and rate (1–5 stars).
  • Messages — text messages, replies, and emoji reactions you send in group and friend chats.
  • Voice notes — audio recordings in chat are stored locally on your device only and are not uploaded to our servers.

Device & Technical Data

  • Push notification token — an Expo push token used to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Device platform — whether you use iOS or Android, stored alongside your push token.

Third-Party Sign-In

  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We do not receive or store your Google password.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide & personalize the App — deliver daily finds based on your interests, match you with discussion groups, and operate chat features.
  • Authentication — verify your identity via phone OTP or Google sign-in.
  • Push notifications — send you daily find deliveries, friend requests, and chat activity alerts.
  • Friend connections — enable you to add friends by phone number and send invitations.
  • Improve the service — use aggregated, de-identified ratings data to improve content curation.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With other users — your first name, last name, and avatar are visible to group members and friends within the App. Messages you send are visible to members of the same chat.
  • Service providers — we use the following third-party services to operate the App:
    • Supabase — authentication, database, file storage, and real-time features.
    • Expo / EAS — push notification delivery and app build services.
    • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notification delivery infrastructure.
    • Google — OAuth sign-in provider.
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on servers provided by Supabase with encryption at rest and in transit. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using platform-native secure storage (Keychain on iOS, EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android). We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no system is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Chat messages are retained for the lifetime of the associated group or friend chat.
  • If you delete your account, your profile, messages, ratings, and push tokens are permanently removed from our systems.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & update — you can view and edit your profile information at any time in the App.
  • Delete your account — you can request account deletion within the App. This permanently deletes all your data.
  • Push notifications — you can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.
  • Camera & photo library — you can revoke these permissions in your device settings. The App will fall back to default emoji avatars.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it.

8. International Users

Your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. By using the App, you consent to such transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy in the App with a new effective date. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
