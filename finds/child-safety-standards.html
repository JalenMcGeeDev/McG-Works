Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: April 2, 2026

Finds - Daily Discovery ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Find mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and take a zero-tolerance approach to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. This document describes our standards, preventative measures, enforcement practices, and reporting procedures with respect to CSAE.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

Find has a strict zero-tolerance policy toward any content, conduct, or communication that sexually exploits or abuses minors. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — any image, video, text, or other content that sexually depicts or exploits a minor.
  • Grooming — any attempt to build trust with a minor for the purpose of sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Solicitation — any request directed at a minor for sexual content, images, or contact.
  • Sextortion — threatening or coercing a minor to produce or share sexual content.
  • Age misrepresentation — falsely claiming to be a minor or adult for the purpose of exploiting or gaining inappropriate access to minors.
  • Sharing or distributing any material that facilitates CSAE, including links, files, or instructions.

Any account found engaging in such conduct will be immediately suspended and permanently terminated. We report all discovered CSAM and related conduct to the appropriate authorities as described below.

2. Prevention Measures

We design Find with safeguards that reduce the risk of CSAE from the ground up:

  • Invite-only access — Find is an invite-only application. New users can only join through an invitation from an existing member, limiting anonymous or unvetted access.
  • Mandatory authentication — all users must authenticate via phone number one-time password (OTP) or Google sign-in. Fully anonymous accounts are not permitted.
  • Real-name profiles — users are required to provide their first and last name during onboarding. Profiles are visible within their group and friend connections.
  • Age restriction — Find is not intended for children under the age of 13. Users under 18 are subject to additional restrictions consistent with applicable law. We do not knowingly permit accounts for children under 13.
  • Limited public exposure — user profiles and messages are not publicly searchable. Content is only visible to invited group members and confirmed friends.

3. User Reporting

We empower users to report CSAE and other harmful conduct directly within the App. Any user can:

  • Use the in-app report button on any message, profile, or content to flag it for immediate review.
  • Contact our safety team directly by email at jalen@mcg-works.com with the subject line "Safety Report."

Reports are treated with strict confidentiality. We do not retaliate against users who make good-faith safety reports.

4. Moderation & Enforcement

Upon receiving a report or otherwise becoming aware of potential CSAE:

  • The reported account is reviewed promptly.
  • Content that violates this policy is removed immediately.
  • Accounts found in violation are suspended immediately pending investigation, and permanently terminated upon confirmation of a violation.
  • All relevant data (messages, account details, device information) is preserved for potential law enforcement disclosure.

Violations of this policy may also constitute violations of federal and state law. We treat every potential CSAE incident with the seriousness it demands.

5. Mandatory Reporting to NCMEC

We comply fully with 18 U.S.C. § 2258A (the PROTECT Our Children Act). Upon discovering apparent child sexual abuse material (CSAM) on our platform, we will:

  • Immediately remove the content from our platform.
  • Report the content to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline at missingkids.org/cybertipline.
  • Preserve all associated account data and content as required by law.

Failure to report known CSAM is a federal crime. We take this obligation seriously and will not hesitate to report, even if doing so results in account termination and legal consequences for the violating user.

6. Law Enforcement Cooperation

We cooperate fully with lawful requests from law enforcement agencies investigating CSAE. This includes:

  • Responding to valid subpoenas, court orders, and search warrants in a timely manner.
  • Voluntarily disclosing information to law enforcement in emergency situations where there is an imminent risk of harm to a child, consistent with 18 U.S.C. § 2258B.
  • Preserving records upon legal hold or government request.

7. Continuous Improvement

We are committed to continuously reviewing and improving our child safety practices. This includes:

  • Evaluating new technical tools and industry best practices for CSAE detection and prevention.
  • Reviewing and updating this policy as our platform evolves.
  • Training staff and contractors who interact with user content on CSAE recognition and response procedures.
