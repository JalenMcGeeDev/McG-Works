CoGoals ("we," "us," or "our") operates the CoGoals mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our App.

1.3 Information Accessed but Not Stored

  • Contacts: When you choose to invite someone to a group, we access your device contacts to let you select a phone number. We do not upload, store, or sync your full contact list. Only the phone number of the person you invite is saved to send the invitation.
  • Camera and Photo Library: Used to take or select photos for your profile avatar or action log attachments. We only access photos you explicitly select.
  • Microphone: Used for audio features within the App. Audio is only accessed when you initiate recording.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • Display your profile and activity to members of your groups
  • Track goal progress and calculate XP, streaks, and levels
  • Send push notifications for check-in reminders, teammate activity, due dates, and milestone celebrations
  • Generate AI-powered action suggestions for your goals (goal titles are sent to our server-side AI service; your personal data is not included)
  • Enable GIF search for reactions (search queries are sent to GIPHY)

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored on Supabase's cloud infrastructure with row-level security policies ensuring users can only access data within their own groups.
  • Authentication is handled via SMS OTP — we do not store passwords.
  • Profile photos and action attachments are stored in secure cloud storage buckets with scoped access policies.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

  • Your account data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • Group invitation records expire after 30 days.
  • If you delete your account, your profile data and personal content will be removed. Shared group content (e.g., group activity history) may be retained in anonymized form.

6. Children's Privacy

CoGoals is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for optional features (e.g., push notifications, contacts access)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@cogoal.app.

8. Analytics and Tracking

CoGoals does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not serve ads and do not share your data with advertisers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App or by other means. The "Effective Date" at the top indicates when the policy was last revised.
