Section 1

Executive Summary

Two Beaches Baking is a food, drink, and storytelling brand built around real recipes and the stories behind them, from two former reporters and longtime friends who cook, bake, and write. This proposal covers the design and build of a website that gives Two Beaches Baking a permanent home for their recipes and blog content, independent of Instagram and Facebook, with the discovery, sharing, and monetization features that established recipe and food blogs rely on.

The site is organized around two core content types, Recipes and Blog posts, plus supporting pages (About, Contact, and a Home page that highlights featured content). Every recipe page pairs a narrative "story" section (the backstory, memory, or context Two Beaches Baking is known for) with a structured, printable recipe card, including a Jump to Recipe button so time-pressed visitors can skip straight to the ingredients and steps, and a print/PDF export option so visitors can save or print recipes for their own kitchen.

Section 2

Project Goals & Success Criteria

  • Give Two Beaches Baking a professional, on-brand website that reflects the "adventurous home cooks / former reporters who love a good story" voice.
  • Make it easy for visitors to find, read, and use recipes, including printing or exporting them for offline use.
  • Support the storytelling format Two Beaches Baking is built on: every recipe has a narrative section, not just a bare ingredient list.
  • Let visitors create a free account so they can save/favorite recipes and build their own personal recipe box on the site, encouraging repeat visits.
  • Give the Two Beaches Baking Admin a content management system they can use themselves, without a developer, to publish new recipes and blog posts.

Section 4

Site Structure & How It Works

4.1 Core Pages

  • Home: Featured/recent recipes and blog posts, brand intro, links to social channels.
  • Recipes: Browsable, filterable index of all recipes (by category, e.g., breakfast, dinner, dessert, drinks; and by tag, e.g., beach-friendly, make-ahead, gluten-free).
  • Blog: Browsable index of blog/story posts, separate from (but cross-linked with) recipes.
  • About: Two Beaches Baking's story, photos, brand voice ("adventurous home cooks in NC, former reporters who love a good story").
  • Contact: Simple contact form and/or email, plus social links.
  • Individual Recipe Page: Story + recipe card (see Section 7).
  • Individual Blog Post Page: Standard blog post template with images, categories/tags, and related content.

4.2 Visitor Flow

1

Discover the Site

Visitor lands on the site via Google search, Pinterest, Instagram link, or direct visit.

2

Browse or Search

Visitor browses the Recipes or Blog page, or searches for a specific recipe/topic.

3

Read the Story or Jump to the Recipe

Visitor opens a recipe page, reads the story, or taps Jump to Recipe to go straight to the card.

4

Print, Save, or Share

Visitor can print the recipe, download it as a PDF, or share it to social media/Pinterest.

5

Save to an Account

Visitor can create a free account (or log in) to save the recipe to their personal "My Recipes" page for later.

4.3 Site Owner (Admin) Flow

1

Log Into the Dashboard

The Two Beaches Baking Admin logs into the CMS admin dashboard.

2

Create a Recipe

Creates a new recipe using a recipe template: story/intro, ingredients, instructions, notes/tips, photos, prep/cook time, servings, category/tags.

3

Create a Blog Post

Creates a new blog post using a standard post editor: title, body, photos, category/tags.

4

Publish or Save as Draft

Publishes, schedules, or saves the recipe or post as a draft.

5

Manage Existing Content

Manages existing content (edit, update photos, reorganize categories) at any time.

6

Check Visit Statistics

Checks the admin dashboard for visit statistics (traffic over time and top recipes/posts) to see what's resonating with readers.

Section 6

Functional Requirements

6.1 Recipes

  • Recipe index page with browse/filter by category (e.g., Breakfast, Mains, Desserts, Drinks) and tags (e.g., quick, make-ahead, beach food, gluten-free).
  • Individual recipe template with:
    • Story/backstory section (freeform rich text + photos)
    • Jump to Recipe button/link that scrolls straight to the recipe card
    • Structured, printable recipe card: title, photo, prep/cook/total time, servings (with serving-size adjustment if desired, see open decisions), ingredient list, step-by-step instructions, notes/tips
    • Print button and Export/Download as PDF button
    • Recipe categories and tags
    • Related recipes module ("You might also like")
  • Recipe schema/structured data (Recipe rich snippet markup) so recipes are eligible for Google recipe search features and rich Pinterest pins.
  • Recipe card designed to look good when pinned to Pinterest (clear, on-brand recipe image).

6.2 Blog

  • Blog index page with category/tag filtering and pagination.
  • Individual blog post template: title, hero image, body content (rich text, embedded images), categories/tags, related posts.
  • Optional: comments on blog posts (see Section 13 open decisions).

6.3 About & Contact

  • About page with founder bios/photos and the Two Beaches Baking brand story.
  • Contact page with a simple form (name, email, message) that emails the site owners, plus links to Instagram/Facebook/Threads.

6.4 Accounts & Saved Recipes

  • Free visitor account creation via email/password (and optionally Google/Facebook login).
  • "Save" or "favorite" button on every recipe card (and optionally on blog posts).
  • Personal "My Recipes" / "My Saved Recipes" dashboard page showing everything the visitor has saved, with the ability to remove items.
  • Basic account management: password reset, update email, delete account.
  • Saved recipes persist across devices as long as the visitor is logged in (not just stored locally in their browser).

6.5 Site-Wide Features

  • Global search across recipes and blog posts.
  • Category/tag taxonomy shared (or separately managed) across recipes and blog.
  • Social sharing buttons (Pinterest, Facebook, Instagram link, email) on every recipe and blog post.
  • Instagram feed embed (pulls recent posts from @twobeachesbaking onto the site).
  • Mobile-responsive design across all pages and templates.

6.6 Admin Dashboard & Site Analytics

  • Private, login-protected admin dashboard (separate from the public site) for the Two Beaches Baking Admin to manage content and check on how the site is doing.
  • Content management: create/edit/publish/unpublish recipes and blog posts, manage categories/tags, upload photos.
  • Visit statistics visible right in the dashboard, without needing a separate analytics account, including:
    • Total site visits/page views over time (day/week/month view)
    • Top-performing recipes and blog posts (by save count)
  • User account list (registered visitors), for basic oversight, not for the client to view individual user activity beyond aggregate stats.
Section 7

Recipe Page Anatomy (Story + Recipe Card)

Every recipe page is built around the two-part structure Two Beaches Baking is known for: a story up top, a usable recipe below.

SectionPurpose
Story / BackstoryFreeform narrative: memory, context, tips, why the recipe matters. Supports photos, formatting, and personality. This is what separates a Two Beaches Baking recipe from a generic recipe listing.
Jump to RecipeA button placed near the top of the story that scrolls the visitor straight down to the recipe card, standard practice on recipe blogs and expected by most visitors.
Recipe CardStructured, consistently formatted block: title, photo, prep/cook/total time, yield/servings, ingredient list, numbered instructions, and optional notes. Visually distinct from the story section (bordered card, print-friendly layout).
Print / PDF ExportButtons on the recipe card that generate a clean, printable version (and a downloadable PDF) with just the recipe (no story, ads, or site navigation) so it's easy to use in the kitchen or save for later.
Notes & TipsOptional callouts for substitutions, storage instructions, or make-ahead tips.

Technology Approach

LayerApproach
Website/FrontendCustom-built site (Next.js) hosted on Vercel
Backend, Database & AuthSupabase: database, visitor account authentication (saved recipes), and file storage for recipe/blog photos
Admin DashboardCustom-built, login-protected admin dashboard (hosted alongside the main site) for managing recipes, blog posts, and viewing visit statistics, built specifically for Two Beaches Baking rather than an off-the-shelf CMS plugin
Recipe FunctionalityCustom-built recipe card component: story + card layout, Jump to Recipe, print/PDF export, and recipe schema markup for Google/Pinterest
Accounts & Saved RecipesSupabase Auth (email/password, optionally Google/Facebook login) with saved recipes stored per account in the Supabase database
Site AnalyticsLightweight, privacy-friendly analytics (e.g., Vercel Analytics or Plausible) feeding visit/traffic data into the admin dashboard
Transactional EmailResend for account-related emails (e.g., signup confirmation, password reset)
Free tier now, usage-based cost as you grow: Vercel, Supabase, and Resend all offer generous free tiers, and this site is expected to run on those free tiers at launch, meaning no hosting/database/email cost to the client at the start. However, all three are usage-based: if traffic, storage (photos), bandwidth, database size, or transactional email volume grow enough (a very good problem to have, since it means the site is getting real traffic and readers), one or more may require upgrading to a paid plan. Typical starting paid tiers for Vercel and Supabase run in the $20–$25/month range each; Resend's paid tiers typically start lower (roughly $20/month once free email volume is exceeded). McG Works LLC will flag proactively if usage is approaching free-tier limits so this is never a surprise bill.

Section 13

Assumptions & Open Decisions

Assumptions

  • Recipes and blog posts are the two primary content types; both are manageable by the client without developer help via a custom-built admin dashboard.
  • The site is custom-built (Next.js on Vercel, Supabase for database/auth) rather than a WordPress/off-the-shelf CMS, to support the custom recipe card, accounts, and analytics dashboard as one integrated system.
  • Vercel and Supabase are expected to run on their free tiers at launch ($0/month); the client understands that usage-based growth (traffic, storage, database size) may require upgrading to paid tiers over time (see Section 10 and Section 11).
  • Client will supply their own recipe content, photos, and About page bios/photos (or McG Works LLC can advise on photography needs).
  • Free visitor accounts (for saving/favoriting recipes) are included in this engagement; there is no paid membership, paywalled content, or e-commerce/ad network integration.
  • Client will own/purchase their domain; Vercel/Supabase account ownership is addressed in Section 12.

Open Decisions (to finalize at kickoff)

  • Recipe categories and tags: confirm the initial list (course, cuisine, dietary, occasion, etc.)
  • Whether comments will be enabled on recipes and/or blog posts
  • Whether recipes should support serving-size scaling (i.e., visitor adjusts servings and ingredient amounts auto-scale)
  • Whether nutrition information will be displayed (and if so, whether it's manually entered or auto-calculated)
  • Whether the Vercel/Supabase accounts are set up under the client's own billing (recommended for ownership) or under McG Works' accounts (simpler if going with the 360 Care Plan)
  • Analytics tool choice for the admin dashboard (e.g., Vercel Analytics vs. Plausible)
  • Whether "print/PDF export" should also allow the visitor to hide/show photos, or strip to text-only
  • Whether account creation should be email/password only, or also support "Continue with Google/Facebook" social login

Future Potential Capabilities (Post-Launch, Not in This Scope)

  • Ad network integration once traffic thresholds are met
  • Affiliate link/product recommendation blocks (e.g., Amazon affiliate cookware links)
  • Recipe ratings/reviews with visitor-submitted star ratings
  • Expanded recipe box features (meal planning, shopping list generation, recipe collections/folders)
  • Cookbook or merchandise sales page
  • Multi-author support if the blog expands beyond the Two Beaches Baking Admin
