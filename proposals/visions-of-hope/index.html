Section 1

Overview

This proposal outlines the development of a clean, professional two-page website for Visions of Hope Counseling Services. The site will serve as an informative online presence for individuals and couples seeking counseling, DWI services, substance assessments, and related support — with a direct link to your existing scheduling portal.

The website is designed to feel calm, trustworthy, and welcoming — reflecting the care and professionalism Visions of Hope brings to every client.

Section 2

Scope of Work

Page 1 — Home

  • Practice name, tagline, and welcoming introduction
  • Brief overview of who you serve (individuals, couples, groups)
  • Prominent "Schedule an Appointment" call-to-action button linking to your scheduling portal
  • Contact information and location details
  • Professional, calming design appropriate for a counseling practice

Page 2 — Services

Full detail on each of the following services:

  • Individual Counseling
  • Couples Counseling
  • DWI Services
  • Substance Assessments
  • DMV Evaluations
  • SAP (Substance Abuse Professional) Assessments
  • Outpatient Group Therapy

Scheduling Integration

  • A clearly visible "Schedule an Appointment" button/link on both pages
  • Links directly to your existing scheduling portal — no portal is built or managed as part of this project
📝 Content & Copy Required from Client: All written content for both pages — including the practice description, service descriptions, tagline, contact information, and any other text — must be provided by the Visions of Hope team before development begins. McG Works will not write, source, or assume any copy on the client's behalf.

Section 7

What's Not Included

To keep this project simple and within budget, the following are outside the scope of this proposal:

  • Written content and copy — all text for both pages must be provided by the Visions of Hope team
  • Logo design or graphic design assets — client to provide any images or logo
  • Domain name registration — must be purchased separately by the client if a custom web address is desired (e.g. visionsofhopecounseling.com). McG Works does not purchase or manage domains. Domains typically cost $10–20/year, renewed annually.
  • Ongoing maintenance or hosting fees after handoff
  • Building or managing a scheduling portal
  • SEO optimization campaigns
  • Any future content updates after the handoff period
Section 8

Hosting

Your website will be deployed on Vercel, a reliable and developer-friendly hosting platform. Vercel's free tier is well-suited for static websites of this size and includes:

  • Fast global content delivery
  • Automatic HTTPS / SSL security
  • Simple custom domain connection (if you have a domain)

At handoff, the Vercel project will be transferred to an account of your choosing. There are no ongoing hosting fees on the free tier for a site of this scope.
