Section 1

Executive Summary

Taste of BLK operates as a culinary talent agency — connecting clients with vetted chefs for private dinners, cookouts, weddings, corporate events, and festivals. Today, matching a client request to the right chef relies on Jessica’s personal knowledge, a Google Form intake, and manual outreach. As the roster grows and request volume increases, this process doesn’t scale.

McG Works will build an internal Chef Database & Matching Platform that allows the Taste of BLK team to intake client requests and instantly surface the best-fit chefs based on cuisine, budget, guest count, service style, availability, and more. Taste of BLK remains the intermediary at all times — chef contact details stay internal, and clients never interact with the database directly.

StakeholderValue Delivered
Taste of BLK StaffInstantly find and recommend matching chefs for any client request; eliminate manual cross-referencing of spreadsheets and forms
ClientsReceive curated chef options faster, matched to their event needs, budget, and dietary requirements
ChefsGet matched to events that fit their skills, capacity, and pricing — leading to better bookings
Section 2

Project Goals & Success Criteria

Goals

  • Replace manual chef-matching with a searchable, filterable internal database that surfaces top chef recommendations automatically.
  • Protect Taste of BLK’s intermediary role — keep chef contact information internal and prevent clients from bypassing the agency.
  • Reduce the time from client request to chef recommendation from days to minutes.
  • Provide a structured system for managing chef profiles, intake data, availability, and booking history.
  • Support Taste of BLK’s 10% commission model by tracking bookings and revenue through the platform.

Success Criteria

  • Staff can enter a client request and receive 3–5 ranked chef suggestions within seconds.
  • All existing chef intake data (from the Google Form) is imported into the platform.
  • Jasmine and Jessica can manage chef profiles and client requests without technical assistance.
  • Chef contact details are never visible to clients at any point in the workflow.

Section 4

How It Works

4.1 Client Request Intake

1

Client Submits Intake Form

A client fills out an intake form with their event details: event type, date, guest count, budget, desired cuisine, service style, dietary restrictions, and venue constraints. The form submission creates a new request in the platform.

2

Staff Reviews & Enables Request

The Taste of BLK team receives a notification of the new request. A team member reviews the submission for completeness and legitimacy, then enables the request to trigger the matching process.

3

System Surfaces Matches

The platform automatically filters and scores the chef roster, returning the top 3–5 chefs with match reasons (e.g., “cuisine match,” “budget within 10%,” “capacity fits guest count”).

4

Staff Reviews & Curates

The team reviews suggestions, inspects full chef profiles, adjusts filters if needed, and selects 1–3 chefs to present to the client. Staff can also manually include or exclude chefs.

5

Present Options to Client

Staff presents curated chef options to the client (via phone consultation, email, or PDF) — with chef summaries, sample menus, and pricing. Chef direct contact info is never shared.

6

Book & Record

Once a chef is selected, staff records the booking in the platform, creates a contract record, and tracks the 10% Taste of BLK commission.

4.2 Chef Profile Management

1

Add Chef to Database

Staff adds a new chef profile using data from the culinary partner intake form. Existing Google Form responses will be imported during initial setup.

2

Maintain Profiles

Staff can update chef details at any time — new menu items, updated pricing, availability changes, portfolio additions, or internal notes and feedback from past events.

3

Track Performance

Internal notes, reliability scores, and past event feedback are stored on each chef’s profile to inform future matching decisions.

Section 7

Core Data Model

6.1 Chef Profile

Each chef profile is stored privately and visible only to Taste of BLK staff.

  • Identity: Display name / business name, short bio, specialties
  • Contact: Email, phone — hidden from clients, visible to Admin & Booker only
  • Cuisine Types: Multi-select tags (e.g., Southern, Caribbean, Italian, Vegan, BBQ)
  • Signature Dishes / Sample Menu: Text descriptions + price-per-person ranges
  • Service Styles: Plated, buffet, grazing table, passed apps, food truck, on-site catering
  • Capacity: Minimum and maximum guest counts the chef can handle
  • Staffing & Equipment: Chef-only vs. requires servers; preferred staff count. Equipment needs (stove, fryer, grill, power, tent, etc.)
  • Dietary Accommodations: Tags for vegan, gluten-free, halal, kosher, allergy-friendly, etc.
  • Pricing: Pricing model (per guest, flat fee, packages) + sample rates / quote history
  • Availability: General availability notes and lead time required
  • Geography: Service area and travel constraints
  • Portfolio: Photos, PDF menus, and references
  • Paperwork: Contract status, W9, insurance (boolean + attachments)
  • Internal Notes: Reliability score, past event feedback, team observations

6.2 Client Request

  • Client Info: Contact name, email, phone
  • Event Details: Event type (private dinner, cookout, wedding, corporate, festival), date/time, guest count
  • Budget: Total budget or per-guest budget, willingness to negotiate
  • Cuisine & Menu: Desired cuisine types, specific menu requests or examples
  • Service Style: Preferred service style (plated, buffet, passed apps, etc.)
  • Dietary Restrictions: Any dietary or allergy requirements
  • Venue Constraints: Indoor/outdoor, on-site kitchen availability, power access
  • Priority & Timeline: Request urgency and event timeline
Section 8

Matching & Scoring Logic

When a client request is submitted, the system filters and ranks chefs using a weighted scoring model.

Filter Sequence

Chefs are filtered in the following order (fast-pass elimination):

  • Availability on the requested date
  • Capacity fits guest count
  • Service style match
  • Cuisine tag match
  • Geography / travel constraints
  • Lead time is sufficient
  • Budget compatibility (chef’s minimum ≤ client budget)
  • Equipment / venue constraints compatible

Scoring Weights

FactorWeight
Availability30%
Capacity / Guest Count20%
Service Style15%
Cuisine Match15%
Budget Match10%
Equipment / Venue Fit10%

Fallback Rules

  • If fewer than 3 chefs match, relax budget constraints first, then cuisine specificity, then allow chefs with partial availability (requires staff confirmation).
  • Staff can manually override any filter, include or exclude specific chefs, and finalize the list before presenting to the client.

Audit Trail

Every match result is stored — including the scores, filters applied, and any staff overrides — so the team can review past decisions and refine the matching logic over time.

Section 12

Assumptions & Open Decisions

12.1 Assumptions

  • Initial chef roster is small (under 50 chefs); the database is designed to scale to 150+ chefs.
  • Taste of BLK remains the intermediary for all client-chef interactions — chef contact info is never exposed to clients.
  • The 10% commission model is tracked in the platform but payments and payouts are handled off-platform for the MVP (Option A from the tech spec).
  • Jessica will share the existing culinary partner intake Google Form and chef responses for data import.
  • Jasmine will serve as the primary point of contact for requirements and feedback during development.
  • Target rollout: June–July 2026.

12.2 Open Decisions (to finalize at kickoff)

  • Confirm the full list of cuisine type tags and service style options
  • Finalize matching weight priorities based on Taste of BLK’s real-world experience
  • Confirm which fields from the Google Form intake map to chef profile fields
  • Confirm if any additional Admin permission levels are needed within the team
  • Confirm email provider preferences for templated chef outreach

12.3 Future Capabilities (Post-Launch)

The following features are not in scope for this engagement but have been discussed and can be added as future work:

  • Stripe Connect payouts: On-platform payments where clients pay through the website and Taste of BLK’s 10% commission is applied automatically, with chef payouts via Stripe Connect.
  • Availability calendar sync: Integration with Google Calendar so chef availability updates automatically.
  • Public client intake portal: A public-facing form on the Taste of BLK website where clients can submit requests directly (feeding into the matching engine).
  • Chef self-service portal: A login for chefs to update their own profiles, pricing, availability, and portfolio.
  • Analytics dashboard: Trends in request volume, booking rates, revenue by chef, popular cuisines, and seasonal demand.
